## Design Specificity Verdict

Split verdict: the marketing wrapper is specific (four hand-matched kawaii collection illustrations); the product payload is generic (every Best Sellers / Anniversary catalog card renders the identical placeholder illustration regardless of price/name).

Deterministic scan: 31 static findings (10 low-contrast, 9 overused-font, 8 skipped-heading, 1 undersized-ui-text, 1 layout-transition, 1 flat-type-hierarchy, 1 broken-image). Live browser pass added cream-palette / kicker-above-heading / heading-rhythm hits only the runtime detector checks.

False positives ruled out: the broken-image hit (empty lightbox `<img>`) is populated at runtime before display. 3 of 10 low-contrast hits were cross-media-query artifacts (dark-mode value vs light-mode background, never rendered together). overused-font(Inter) doesn't account for Inter being the deliberate body font in a 3-font system.

Convergent finding: both assessments independently calculated `--color-fg-faint` (#A8978D) against cream/white at ~2.6-2.8:1 — one from source token math, one from live-rendered measurement. Real failure, used on the cart's "Remove" link.

No human-visible overlay tab existed this run (no native browser tool exposed to the evidence sub-agent; it used a programmatic Playwright fallback, disclosed not silent).

## Overall Impression

Brand system (illustrations, color, type, checkout error handling) is well above a typical AI-generated storefront. Score is held down by two concrete defects: wrong category headers, and placeholder-only product grid.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Every category page header says "All Products" instead of its own category**
Why it matters: next screen after the highest-intent click on the homepage; reads as a broken link.
Fix: give each collection page its own eyebrow/h1/subtext, sourced from COLLECTIONS data.
Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

**[P1] Every product card in Best Sellers and the Anniversary catalog shows the identical placeholder image**
Why it matters: primary conversion instrument contradicts the page's own copy; forces reading labels instead of browsing.
Fix: real photography is the actual fix (already in progress — Sanrio Bouquet has one); consider scoping Best Sellers to photo-having items only until more exist.
Suggested command: /impeccable polish

**[P2] Checkout modal silently discards all typed data on accidental dismiss**
Why it matters: scrim/Esc/X close the form with zero warning at the highest-intent moment, losing 6 fields of typed data.
Fix: guard close handlers with a "discard changes?" confirmation once any field is non-empty.
Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P2] --color-fg-faint fails WCAG AA contrast on functional text**
Why it matters: ~2.6-2.8:1, confirmed two independent ways; used on the cart's "Remove" link, footer legal line, form helper notes.
Fix: darken the token or restrict it to purely decorative text; --muted-brown already passes at ~4.6:1.
Suggested command: /impeccable audit

**[P3] No persistent "Shop All" entry point in primary nav**
Why it matters: decisive shoppers have no one-click path to the full catalog from most pages.
Fix: add a direct nav item, still within the <=5-item budget.
Suggested command: /impeccable layout
