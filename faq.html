Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a balloon bouquet and an installation?

Balloon bouquets are smaller arrangements that are perfect for birthdays, gifts, table displays, entrances, and other special moments.

Balloon installations are larger displays such as garlands, arches, columns, and backdrops that are designed specifically for your event space.

Installations may also include delivery and on-site setup depending on the order.

Can I customize the colours or theme?

Of course!

We love creating balloon designs that match your celebration. You can share your preferred colours, theme, inspiration photos, or any special details when placing your order.

We'll work with you to make sure your balloons fit your vision as closely as possible!

How far in advance should I place my order?

We require at least 2 business days' notice for all orders so we have enough time to make everything perfect for you!

For larger or more customized setups, we recommend reaching out a little earlier to help ensure availability.

Need something last minute? Send us a message on WhatsApp or through our socials and we'll do our best to make it happen!

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

Yes! We currently offer pickup in Richmond Hill and Mississauga.

We also offer paid delivery to surrounding areas including Markham, Scarborough, Vaughan, Toronto, North York, Etobicoke, Brampton, Oakville, Aurora, and Newmarket.

Delivery fees vary depending on your location and the size of your order.

Don't see your city listed? Send us a message. We may still be able to deliver to you!

Do you deliver outside your regular service area?

Possibly!

While we mainly service Toronto, York Region, Peel Region, and nearby areas, we may be able to travel a little farther for your event for an additional delivery fee.

Send us your location and event details and we'll let you know what we can do!

Do you offer setup?

Yes! For larger balloon installations, we can provide delivery and on-site setup.

Let us know your event location, date, and preferred setup time when submitting your request, and we'll confirm all the details with you.

How does ordering and payment work?

Submitting an order through our website sends us your event and design request.

Once we receive it, we'll review everything and contact you to confirm availability, final pricing, and any pickup, delivery, or setup details.

We currently accept e-transfer and cash.

Your order will be confirmed once the required payment or deposit has been received.

Can I change or cancel my order?

We understand that plans can change!

Cancellations must be made at least 2 business days before your scheduled pickup, delivery, or event date.

If you'd like to make changes to your order, please reach out as soon as possible and we'll do our best to accommodate them.

Changes requested close to your event date may be limited depending on how far along we are in preparing your order.

How long do balloons last?

How long your balloons last depends on the type of balloon and where they're kept.

Indoor balloon arrangements generally last longer when kept away from direct sunlight, heat, sharp objects, and major temperature changes.

For the best results, we recommend keeping your balloons indoors in a comfortable, temperature-controlled space.

If you're unsure when to schedule your pickup or delivery, just let us know your event date and we'll be happy to recommend the best timing!
